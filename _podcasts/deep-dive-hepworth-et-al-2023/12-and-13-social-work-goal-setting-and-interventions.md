  Picking up where assessment leaves off, this episode follows the move into the change-oriented phase of the helping process, from agreeing on goals to choosing how to reach them. The hosts start with client-driven goals and SMART criteria, then work through the goal types the chapter lays out: long-term and short-term, approach and avoidance, learning and performance, and empowerment set against compliance and conformity. They spend time on mandated clients, explaining how to clarify the mandate and when to use an agreeable mandate, "let's make a deal," or getting rid of the mandate, and why keeping to three goals or fewer matters. After discussing what a contract should contain and why it supports informed consent, they turn to interventions: the task-centered model and its task planning and implementation sequence, solution-focused questions, motivational interviewing, cognitive behavioral therapy with attention to environmental realities, and trauma-informed care. Listeners will come away with a clearer picture of how goals, contracts, and interventions connect.
