  Following a single student case from start to finish, this episode walks through the direct practice work of a school social worker as Section I of Chapter 9 lays it out. It begins with the referral process, both formal and informal, and the pre-referral safeguards such as response to intervention that come before a formal referral. It then makes the case that engagement has to come before assessment, using the chapter's example of a reluctant junior high student who opens up once she is asked about dance instead of homework. From there the hosts turn to culturally responsive assessment through a person-in-environment lens, using informal observation and the culturagram rather than generic cultural assumptions. They take up the tension between the NASW's evidence-based practice standards and how little of that research reflects diverse school populations, then cover cognitive behavioral therapy, CBITS for trauma, and the solution-focused brief approach's miracle question and scaling. Listeners will come away with a clearer sense of when to adapt an intervention, and when a student's needs call for school-linked services.
