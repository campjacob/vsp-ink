  This episode was generated using Gemini Notebook from Chapter 4, "Ethical Responsibilities in Practice Settings," and Chapter 5, "Ethical Responsibilities as Professionals," in Reamer's Ethical Standards in Social Work: A Review of the NASW Code of Ethics. It is designed to help you engage with and review the material, not to replace the reading.


  Social workers do not practice alone, and this episode looks at the ethical standards that govern the agencies they work in and the professionals they are. It begins with supervisors and administrators, including the need to avoid dual relationships with supervisees and an administrator's duty to fix conditions such as staff gossiping about clients or inadequate consent forms. The hosts then turn to conflicts between employer commitments and ethical practice, following social workers in a juvenile correctional facility, a middle school, and a school-based clinic without after-hours coverage who used the code to change agency practice, along with how the code treats strikes. The second half covers responsibilities as professionals: competence, discrimination, private conduct, impairment and seeking help, misrepresenting credentials, and soliciting vulnerable clients. The episode closes by asking how social workers will rely on their own judgment where the code gives no clear answer.
